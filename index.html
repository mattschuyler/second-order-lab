    Step response

    Overdamped · ζ > 1

    α > ω₀. Two distinct real roots s± = −α ± √(α² − ω₀²).

    yh(t) = A es₊t + B es₋t

    Slow and sluggish. The root nearer the origin sets how long it takes.

    Critically damped · ζ = 1

    α = ω₀. One repeated real root s = −α.

    yh(t) = A est + B t est

    The fastest response that never overshoots.

    Underdamped · ζ < 1

    α < ω₀. Complex roots −α ± jωd, with ωd = √(ω₀² − α²).

    yh(t) = e−αt(A cos ωdt + B sin ωdt)

    Rings at ωd inside a decaying e−αt envelope and overshoots.
